{{<lang "en">}}

Years ago, I stumbled upon [lpla's syncross](https://github.com/lpla/synccross), a fork itself inspired by [rebbTRSi](https://github.com/rebbTRSi/synccross-LSDj-1-to-3-adapter-).

The first shocking news after discovering this project was that I couldn't use it because the **syncross** in question only supported the *GameBoy Color* connector.

This sparked a fun DIY session, where I carefully measured the size of the DMG-04 link cable input. All it took was the correct measurements on KiDad, and voilà, "the Synchronizer!"

![image](/media/content/synchronizer2.png)

Obviously, it was necessary to brand the circuit board with a couple of decals to reiterate our identity, and, on the wise advice of Ick_ and Artemisia666, an elegant hole to make it more wearable.
